3. Key System Features & How They Work
• Farmer Daily Reporting Portal
Simple Guided Entry: Farmers follow a clear step-by-step form to record daily bird mortality, culling, feed consumption, egg collection, house temperature, and body weights.
Offline Mode & Protection: If mobile network or internet is lost at the farm, report data is safely saved on the phone and automatically synced to the cloud when internet connection restores.
Multilingual Support (English & Tamil): Farmers can easily toggle between English and Tamil with a single tap for seamless daily usage.
Same-Day Revisions: If a mistake is made during data entry, farmers can correct today's submission while keeping a secure audit trail.
• Supervisor Monitoring & Operations
Real-Time Farm Status: Field supervisors see instant status dashboards showing whether each assigned farm has submitted its report for the day.
Flock & Inventory Tracking: Track flock age in weeks, cumulative mortality rates, and remaining feed stock for every farm in real-time.
Performance Rankings & Comparisons: Compare farm performance side-by-side to quickly identify top-performing units or farms needing intervention.
Feed Delivery Management: Record incoming feed stock deliveries to automatically update and balance farm feed inventories.
• Executive Admin Management & Analytics
Central Executive Dashboard: At-a-glance oversight of total bird population, daily egg collection totals, mortality rates, and overall feed efficiency.
User & Access Administration: Create new farmer accounts, manage active/disabled user statuses, and assign farm access permissions.
Flock Lifecycle Tracking: Register new flocks, configure bird breeds, and track flock age automatically throughout its lifecycle.
Predictive Trends & Analytics: Intelligent data calculations compute moving averages and production trends to help forecast egg yields and spot health concerns early.
4. Testing Steps & Feedback Collection
1. Test Farmer Entry: Log in via the Farmer Portal on mobile or browser and try submitting a test daily report.
2. Test Offline Sync: Disconnect your internet connection, submit a report, reconnect, and observe automatic sync.
3. Test Management Dashboard: Log in via the Management Portal to inspect live farm cards, rankings, and analytics.
4. Share Your Feedback: Please write down any observations, questions, or desired improvements and share them with our team so we can promptly refine the system for your operations.
